## Overview

This work investigates how source- and acquisition-related input variations propagate through the XCT measurement chain.

## Research workflow

**Selected input variation → projection → reconstruction → surface determination → dimensional measurand**

## Key contributions

- Used experimentally observed projection-wise acquisition behaviour to motivate controlled simulation studies.
- Prescribed source-related variations while keeping geometry, detector settings, reconstruction, and measurement procedures fixed.
- Evaluated their effects on reconstructed images, surface localization, and task-specific dimensional measurands.
- Developed a simulation-based framework for variation propagation and attribution.

## Methods & tools

**Projection-resolved simulation · FDK reconstruction · Monte Carlo analysis · surface determination · dimensional metrology**
