## Overview

This project investigated how scan strategies influence thermal history and resulting material properties during fused-deposition modelling of PEEK.

## Key contributions

- Developed an integrated simulation–experiment framework for thermal behaviour during FDM.
- Combined finite-element process simulation with **in-situ infrared thermography**.
- Validated transient temperature predictions against experimental monitoring.
- Quantified links between thermal history, crystallinity, and mechanical properties.
- Evaluated simulation-guided scan-strategy design for tailoring high-performance polymer components.

## Characterization

Mechanical testing, thermal analysis, microscopy, and surface characterization were used to assess process–structure–property relationships.
