## Overview

This project develops and evaluates a component-based digital-twin framework for industrial X-ray computed tomography (XCT). The work connects scanner-component modelling and computational imaging with projection-level experimental validation.

## Key contributions

- Reviewed industrial XCT simulation and digital-twin approaches, including modelling of the source, detector, acquisition geometry, manipulator, and scanned object.
- Compared projection data from **three representative simulators** with measurements acquired from **two industrial XCT systems**.
- Evaluated agreement using physically meaningful projection-level quantities rather than relying only on reconstructed-volume appearance.
- Integrated simulation, scanner experiments, and quantitative Python/MATLAB analysis into a reproducible validation workflow.
