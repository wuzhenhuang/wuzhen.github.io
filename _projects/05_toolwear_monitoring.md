## Overview

This project developed a multi-sensor framework for monitoring tool wear during ceramic-backplane machining.

## Key contributions

- Independently conducted CNC machining experiments and machining-process planning.
- Installed sensors and synchronized acquisition of **acoustic-emission, cutting-force, and vibration** signals.
- Characterized tool wear and machined-surface morphology using ultra-depth-of-field microscopy and SEM.
- Correlated sensor-signal evolution with physical wear mechanisms.
- Developed and validated tool-wear monitoring models through signal processing, feature extraction, and machine-learning classification.

## Outputs

The work contributed to a provincial research project and resulted in **two invention patents and three SCI-indexed publications**.
