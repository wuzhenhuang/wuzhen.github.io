## Research Experience

### Doctoral Researcher — Politecnico di Milano
**Nov 2022 — Present · Milan, Italy**

- Developed and experimentally assessed industrial XCT simulation and digital-twin workflows.

- Supported the **Computer-Aided Manufacturing** course through 3D modelling, process planning, CAM workflows, and hands-on machining activities.
