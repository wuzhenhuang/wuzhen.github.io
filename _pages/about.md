I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at **Politecnico di Milano**. My work combines physics-based modelling, experimental measurement, sensing, and quantitative data analysis across **X-ray computed tomography, advanced manufacturing, process monitoring, and materials characterization**.

My current research focuses on computational X-ray CT and simulation-based metrology, including imaging-system modelling, projection simulation, reconstruction, quantitative image analysis, experimental validation, digital-twin development, geometry-consistent reference definition, and uncertainty-oriented measurement.

I previously worked on multi-sensor monitoring of machining processes and on thermal-process simulation and characterization of additively manufactured PEEK. In 2025, I completed a research visit at the **Center for Virtual Imaging Trials, Duke University**, where I adapted computational-imaging methods developed for medical CT to industrial cone-beam XCT.

### Research interests

- **X-ray Imaging & Computational CT:** imaging physics, simulation, reconstruction, quantitative image analysis, digital twins, NDT, and dimensional metrology.
- **Advanced Manufacturing & Process Monitoring:** additive and subtractive manufacturing, thermal monitoring, multi-sensor acquisition, signal processing, and machine learning.
- **Materials Characterization & Quality Assessment:** mechanical and thermal testing, microscopy, surface characterization, dimensional evaluation, and defect assessment.
